About these models

Every model here was trained from scratch: the tokenizer, the pretraining and the chat fine-tuning, with no borrowed weights. They use the same architecture as Meta's Llama (RMSNorm, rotary positions, grouped-query attention, SwiGLU), written in PyTorch and trained on a MacBook and on free Kaggle GPUs.

The model runs on your own device through WebAssembly (int8, transformers.js), so there is no server and your messages never leave your browser.

For scale

These models have 40–100 million parameters. Commercial assistants have hundreds of billions, thousands of times more, and were trained on far more text. Expect fluent writing with unreliable facts.
