# AutoLearn Chat

Chat with small language models that were trained from scratch (tokenizer, pretraining and chat tuning) on a
MacBook and free Kaggle GPUs. The model runs entirely in your browser with
[transformers.js](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers.js): no server, and your messages never leave your device.

Models: [AutoLearn 101M Chat](https://huggingface.co/Likhith003/autolearn-101m-chat) (default) and
[AutoLearn 40M Chat](https://huggingface.co/Likhith003/autolearn-40m-chat) (faster).
Source code: [github.com/likhithy99/autolearn-api](https://github.com/likhithy99/autolearn-api).
